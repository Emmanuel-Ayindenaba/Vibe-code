Help & FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

What file types can I upload?

JPEG, PNG, or PDF invoice templates.

Can I add more than one stamp?

Yes. Each stamp runs its own independent numbering sequence, start number, and interval — click "+ Add stamp" in the Stamps section.

How do I reposition a stamp?

Click a stamp on the invoice preview to select it, then drag it to where you want it.

What output formats are supported?

You can generate a batch as individual PNG images, a single merged PDF, or a zip of all files.

Is my data private?

Everything runs in your browser — invoices are processed locally and are not uploaded to a server.
