Smile Numbered
Invoice numbering, made painless

Smile Numbered helps you stamp sequential numbers onto invoices in bulk: drop in a template, place one or more stamps, and generate a whole batch as PNGs or PDFs in seconds. Built for small businesses, printers, and anyone tired of numbering invoices by hand.

Author

Built and maintained independently by Emmanuel Anallemina, an IT student and software developer passionate about creating simple and useful digital solutions. Smile Numbered was built to make numbering tasks easier, faster, and more efficient.
